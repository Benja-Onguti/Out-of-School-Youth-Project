Who We Are

Turning Hopelessness Into Opportunity

Managed by the Franciscan Missionary Sisters for Africa, the Hands of Care and Hope Initiative serves marginalized communities in Huruma, Madoya, Korogocho, and Kariobangi North.

Our youth program reaches out to 18-26-year-olds who have fallen through the cracks of the education system. Through a mix of vocational training, behavior change mentorship, and youth-led evangelism, we give them the tools to build a career and a better life.

Our Mission Empowering youths holistically through skills and mentorship
Our Vision Eradicating poverty by empowering marginalized youths
